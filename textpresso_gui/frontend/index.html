About MaizeGDB Textpresso

MaizeGDB Textpresso is a literature search engine for maize research, built on the Textpresso text-mining platform. It lets you search the full text of maize papers by free-text keywords, and by categories including maize genes and terms from ontologies including Gene Ontology (GO), Plant Ontology (PO), Trait Ontology (TO) and related ontologies. Category searches find papers where a gene or term has been annotated in the text, even when the exact words you typed do not appear.

Related gene matches

Not all gene synonyms in the literature are exact, one-to-one synonyms. For example, ZmCCT10 has been used as a synonym for both cct1 and cct10. If "Include related gene synonyms" is chosen, results for cct1 will also include results for ZmCCT10; if it is not chosen, those results will be excluded. Therefore, RELATED gene synonyms can return more papers, but also potentially more noise.

Annotated full text viewer

For open-access papers, click a result's title or its "Annotated full text" button to read the whole paper text in a new tab, with figures shown in place (click a figure to enlarge it). The viewer carries over your search, so keyword and category matches are already highlighted. Controls are in the left-hand panel:

  • Keyword matches are highlighted in yellow. Step through them with the ‹ › buttons or the [ and ] keys, or type a new term to find within the paper.
  • Category matches (genes and ontology terms) are highlighted in teal. Step with the buttons or { and }, add other categories annotated in the paper, and optionally include broader or narrower ontology terms and related gene synonyms.
  • Annotations lets you turn highlighting on or off for each ontology or individual term, and jump between occurrences of a chosen term. Click any annotated word in the text to see its categories.
  • Contents jumps to a section or figure.
  • Previous paper / Next paper moves through your search results without returning to the search page.
